# Product

LVTD, LLC is an independent software studio. Its website is the public directory
of the organization's projects, apps, games, research, and writing.

Visitors should be able to identify a project from its name and one sentence,
open it or its source, and move between collections from every page. Preserve
client attribution, archive status, and the original language of game titles.
Describe availability accurately, including work distributed only as source.

The homepage features a selection from the project directory. Games come from
the released arcade registry. Research links to inspectable results and methods.
Blog lists published posts. Keep service pitches and deposit links off the site.
