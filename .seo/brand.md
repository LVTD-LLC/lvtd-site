# LVTD - Brand Context

## Product

- **Name:** LVTD, LLC.
- **One-liner:** Product engineering, automation, and AI workflows from a builder-led product lab.
- **What we do:** Build useful software and operate systems for founders and small teams. Shipped projects demonstrate the work.
- **Pricing:** Named service offers and deposit-led engagements; verify current scope and price before quoting.
- **Free surfaces:** Public writing, project demonstrations, AI steering catalog, and the Jev benchmark. These are not a free service tier.

## Audience

- Technical founders and small teams evaluating hands-on engineering help.
- Operators who need a manual process or unfinished product turned into working software.
- Builders comparing AI workflows who want the actual implementation, results, and limits.
- Jobs: understand a technical tradeoff; inspect proof of shipped work; decide whether LVTD can help.

## Brand voice

- **Tags:** builder-to-builder, direct, technically specific, calm, allergic to hype.
- **Perspective:** "We" for LVTD experiments, "you" for practical takeaways. Use "I" only for attributed founder experience.
- **Rhythm:** Lead with a concrete result or problem. Short paragraphs; useful descriptive headings; one point per section. Explain specialist terms at first use.
- **Forbidden promotional phrases:** "digital transformation", "10x growth", "unlock growth", "scale seamlessly", "end-to-end solutions", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "synergy".
- **Reference content:** `/blog/what-lvtd-builds/`; PRODUCT.md, VISION.md, DESIGN.md. Preserve the practical working-notebook tone.
- **Charts:** Show real data with date, labels, source and a plain-language interpretation. Never decorate unsupported claims with mock metrics.

## Anti-positioning

1. Not a generic AI consultancy.
2. Not fully autonomous delivery without an accountable engineer.
3. Not a passive portfolio with no hiring path.
4. Not a universal model leaderboard or independent benchmark authority.
5. No fabricated clients, timelines, revenue, testimonials or performance guarantees.

## Concrete proof

- Real shipped products and public experiments.
- Django implementation and reusable engineering patterns.
- Documented operations, integrations and workflow decisions.
- Public benchmark questions, responses and comparisons where available.

These are LVTD proof points, not claims of exclusivity versus competitors.

## Proprietary data and first-hand experience

Use verified Jev benchmark snapshots and inspected application behavior. Attribute figures to the exact run and date. Separate observed rankings, interpretation and limitations. A single judge's preference is not objective correctness or broad model superiority. Do not invent engineering anecdotes.
